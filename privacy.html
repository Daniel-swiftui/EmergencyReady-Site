Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 29, 2026

EmergencyReady is designed to help individuals and families organize emergency information, including family details, medical information, emergency plans, meeting places, and emergency alerts.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use EmergencyReady.

Information You Provide

EmergencyReady may store information that you choose to enter, including:

  • Your name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • Family member information
  • Emergency contact information
  • Medical information such as allergies, medications, medical conditions, and blood type
  • Emergency plan information
  • Emergency meeting locations
  • Emergency checklist information
  • Information associated with emergency alerts

Local Profile Information

Your personal EmergencyReady profile, including your name, phone number, and email address, is stored locally on your device.

Your profile may be used to identify you when sending an emergency alert to members of your shared EmergencyReady family.

iCloud and CloudKit

EmergencyReady uses Apple iCloud and CloudKit to synchronize supported emergency information between Apple devices.

If you choose to use EmergencyReady's family-sharing functionality, certain family information may be shared through Apple's CloudKit sharing system with people you explicitly invite.

EmergencyReady does not operate its own external server for storing your family emergency information.

Family Sharing

If you invite another person to your EmergencyReady family, they may be able to access information contained in the shared family, including family member information, medical information, emergency plans, meeting locations, emergency checklists, and emergency alerts.

You are responsible for deciding who you invite to your shared EmergencyReady family.

Medical Information

EmergencyReady may contain medical information that you choose to enter for yourself or family members.

This information is intended only to help organize emergency information. EmergencyReady is not a medical service and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment.

Emergency Alerts

EmergencyReady allows family members to send emergency alerts within a shared family.

Emergency alert information may include the sender's name, the time the alert was sent, and an emergency-alert message.

EmergencyReady may use notifications and Apple's notification services to notify users of emergency alerts.

EmergencyReady does not contact emergency services such as 911 on your behalf unless a feature explicitly states otherwise.

PIN Security

EmergencyReady allows you to create a PIN to protect access to the app.

Your EmergencyReady PIN is stored securely using Apple's Keychain services on your device. EmergencyReady does not store your PIN in CloudKit or share it with members of your EmergencyReady family.

Face ID, Touch ID, and Biometric Authentication

EmergencyReady may allow you to use Face ID, Touch ID, or another supported Apple biometric-authentication method to unlock the app.

Biometric authentication is handled by Apple's operating system. EmergencyReady does not receive, store, or have access to your fingerprint, facial-recognition data, or other biometric information.

Notifications

EmergencyReady may request permission to send notifications. Notification permissions are controlled through iOS Settings and may be disabled at any time.

Tracking and Advertising

EmergencyReady does not use your personal information for targeted advertising.

EmergencyReady does not sell your personal information and does not track your activity across other companies' apps or websites for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

EmergencyReady relies on Apple services including:

  • iCloud
  • CloudKit
  • Keychain
  • LocalAuthentication
  • Apple notification services
  • Apple Maps when requesting directions to an emergency meeting location

Data Retention

Information entered into EmergencyReady remains stored on your device and, where applicable, in your iCloud or CloudKit account until you delete it or remove the associated data.

Deleting Your Data

You can remove editable information directly within EmergencyReady.

Data stored through iCloud or CloudKit may also be managed through your Apple account and iCloud settings.

If you need assistance with deletion or have a privacy-related request, contact danumpi.dev@gmail.com .

Children's Privacy

EmergencyReady is intended to help families organize emergency information. The app does not knowingly collect information from children for advertising, tracking, or marketing purposes.

Adults using EmergencyReady are responsible for information they choose to enter about family members, including minors.

Security

EmergencyReady uses security features provided by Apple, including Keychain, app authentication, and iCloud/CloudKit security.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if EmergencyReady's features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are made.
