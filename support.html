EmergencyReady Support

Need help with EmergencyReady? You can find answers to common questions below or contact support directly.

Family Sharing

EmergencyReady uses Apple iCloud and CloudKit to share supported emergency information with family members you invite.

Notifications

EmergencyReady uses notifications to alert you when a member of your shared family activates Emergency Mode. Notification permissions can be changed in iOS Settings.

Face ID or Touch ID

Biometric unlocking can be enabled or disabled from the Security section inside EmergencyReady Settings. Your device must already have Face ID or Touch ID configured in iOS.

Forgotten PIN

Select "Forgot PIN?" from the PIN entry screen. EmergencyReady will ask you to verify your identity using your device security before allowing you to create a new PIN.
